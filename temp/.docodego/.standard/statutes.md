# the sixteen statutes

Source: docodego.com, "Sixteen principles. Four pillars. One discipline."
Recorded verbatim, 2026-11-06. The site's own enforcement claim: each
principle is violable in a specific way; each violation is observable.
Compiled from the DoCoDeGo Specification (spec/laws, ST-1…16): each
statute binds one stream of the loop, per the arc's Appendix B. Lines
marked † are docodego's completions, recorded honestly beside the
published words.
Statutes 10 and 13 are complete as published, read with a gloss.

## DO — document · the Author (statutes 1–4)
1. Intent Is Absolute — binds validate
   † An emergency patch owes the spec an update within one cycle.
2. The Spec Is the Contract — binds plan
   † Sketches are free until they graduate.
3. Quality Through Context — binds discover
   † Complete context is necessary, never sufficient: prove the output.
4. Simplicity of Spec — binds refine

## CO — compose · the Architect (statutes 5–8)
5. Code Is Regenerable — binds enhance
   † Preserve logic, not syntax. Regenerate from improved intent, or refactor,
   or patch, in proportion to the change. Regenerable never means disposable.
6. Resilience Over Robustness — binds maintain
7. Architecture Before Generation — binds build
8. Comprehension Is Non-Negotiable — binds integrate

## DE — demonstrate · the Gatekeeper (statutes 9–12)
9. Zero-Latency Evolution — binds accept
10. Feedback Closes the Loop — binds demo
11. Observability Is Non-Negotiable — binds deploy
   † You cannot govern what you cannot see. Every demonstrated output includes
   telemetry: the named signal that shows it is working.
12. Atomic Delivery — binds release

## GO — govern · the Recorder (statutes 13–16)
13. The Human as Governor — binds improve
14. Transparent Reasoning — binds retrospect
   † Every agent produces auditable **action** traces. No black boxes: audit
   what it did, not the story it tells.
15. Security by Design — binds monitor
   † 'No new attack surface' is an answer; silence is not.
16. Alignment Before Demonstration — binds measure
   † No demonstration without governance validation at its required maturity
   level, set by its mode. A sketch shown to learn is not a demonstration.

Companion doctrine, verbatim from the site: the DoCoDeGo Axiom — "Intent is
the primary artefact. Implementation is a compiled derivative. Governance is
earned through the loop." IRAF — Intent → Reasoning → Action → Feedback —
replaces the sprint and the release.
