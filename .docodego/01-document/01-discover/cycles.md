- asked: the spec and the shipped tool disagree on the Intent Clarity
  Score's shape — four dimensions out of 100 (the Guide's form, spec
  1.0.1's normative text) or five passes (completeness 25, unambiguity 25,
  testability 25, threat 15, anti-gaming 10 — what `score` ships today)?
  Which is the law?
- found: the founder declined to pick either form and ruled instead (asked
  with a recommendation, answered 2026-11-08, verbatim): "we should always
  strive to improve and do better, nothing is set in stone. ICS in my
  opinion needs a significant uplift and redesign. Same goes for other
  similar scorers grillers etc. We should be willing to add more dimensions
  if they make sense responsibly."
- so: the ICS open question is answered as a commission, not a pick — spec
  7 carries an ICS 2.0 design track (dimensions that make sense, added
  responsibly; the grilling and other gates get the same scrutiny). Until a
  redesign is accepted, the four-dimension form stays normative in the
  spec; the tool's five-pass output is the incumbent being redesigned.
  Recorded in the spec as patch 1.0.2 (status note, no duty change).
