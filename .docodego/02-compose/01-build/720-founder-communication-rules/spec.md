## intent

The founder ruled this session that communication is law, not style,
after a round of back-and-forth clarification questions ("what is an
ADR?" — decision record) that a clear first telling would have saved.
Five laws for every founder-facing word: correct context first (name
the unit, its state, and the evidence before any claim); plain
English (short words over long ones); every acronym spelled out on
first use or not used; concise (the shortest honest form); and every
question answered before implementation begins — a skipped question
is a silent scope decision made by whoever skipped it. Then the
founder widened the audience: "The product needs to be meaningful to
a high school kid and also to a industry veteran" (recorded
verbatim) — the product's own words (help pages, refusal messages,
docs, the public site) must read whole at both ends: every term
introduced before it is used, and nothing dumbed down. The unit
plants these as a rule in the rules tree (`.agents/rules/`), so the
floor validates it like any law: reader, fails-when, enforced-by.
The hop (spec 32 to 33) also carries the AGENTS.md init-bullet
clause deferred from 710 and renames the rules count.

## scope

In: the rule file and its README-map row (payload); AGENTS.md — six
invariant rules named, the init bullet teaching the birth law; the
v32-to-v33 migration teach (anchor-matched, a customized copy keeps
its words and takes the plan's own conflict path); the mirror row;
init's rules-layer comment; pins. Out: mechanical enforcement of
prose style (the rule holds by practice — the grill's first question
and the delivered note read whole; a style scanner would be furniture
pretending to be law); rewording past units' records (their prose is
history); the studio repo's own hard rules (a separate surface).
