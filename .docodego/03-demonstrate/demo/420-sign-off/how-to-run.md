## see it work

One command, the founder's act, end to end:

1. `docodego sign-off <amendment-file> --quote "<words verbatim>"` —
   on a scaffolded amendment, the pending block is replaced with the
   quote verbatim, the law file's sha256, the identity line naming its
   derivation, the scan-clean line, and the attestation limit.
2. `docodego sign-off <file> --quote "sk-…"` — a secret-shaped quote
   is refused with nothing stored (DG-U114).
3. A second sign-off of the same act is refused (DG-U115).
4. `docodego explain DG-U114` — the page for the refusal, the law
   behind it, the fix.
