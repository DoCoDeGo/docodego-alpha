# 03 grademyfit — community fit-grading, no accounts

**Domain:** grademyfit.com · **Price anchor:** $9.99 pro report · **Note:** may run a small local server for persistence; be honest in the record about demo-real vs launch-real.

## Brief (verbatim)

"grademyfit.com is the traffic engine of my portfolio: post an outfit, get graded A–F by the community, with subscores — color harmony, fit and silhouette, occasion-appropriateness, originality — plus consensus notes on what works. Rating other people's fits earns credits toward posting your own; the feed IS the product, and participation is the onboarding. It must work without accounts: identity is a generated handle with a local persona, there's a natural daily rhythm (a free post per day, credits for rating), and an optional paid 'pro fit report' from a style engine for $9.99. The audience arrives daily with a low-stakes but persistent question: 'does this outfit actually work?'"

## Hard lines

- No accounts anywhere: generated handle + local persona is the identity ceiling; optional email only for reply notifications.
- Anti-abuse without accounts is your design problem: one vote per post per session; self-vote, double-vote, replayed-token defenses — proven by tests.
- Moderation minimum ships or nothing ships: NSFW hash-blocklist on uploads, a report button, a human-review queue.
- Public posts are public by consent: state it plainly; nothing beyond what the product needs is stored.
- Vote math and subscores deterministic — same votes, same grades — proven by tests.
- Seed the feed for launch day: the demo must not open empty.
