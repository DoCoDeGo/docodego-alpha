# 02 bodyarchetype — body architecture → flattering cuts

**Domain:** bodyarchetype.com · **Price anchor:** $12.99 one-time

## Brief (verbatim)

"bodyarchetype.com reads a full-body or waist-up photo and names the person's body architecture — the classic families like dramatic, natural, classic, gamine, romantic — then shows which silhouettes, necklines, and garment cuts flatter that architecture. Free: the archetype name, a real taste of the recommendations, and a shareable card. Paid: the full list of what flatters them, $12.99 one-time. The audience arrives with 'nothing fits me right, my proportions are wrong' — the product's entire voice reframes that as architecture to dress, not flaws to fix. Celebratory and neutral, always."

## Hard lines

- TONE LAW: celebratory and neutral. NEVER rank bodies. Never use words like 'flaw', 'problem', 'fix'. Legal-and-brand requirement — enforce with a copy-lint test, not vigilance.
- The photo never leaves the device: analysis fully client-side.
- The archetype classifier must be deterministic and proven by tests.
- Styling guidance only: no medical or diagnosis claims.
