# 00 finessetry — AI color analysis

**Domain:** finessetry.com (owned) · **Price anchor:** one-time ~$9–15

## Brief (verbatim)

"I'm building a color-analysis product for finessetry.com. A visitor uploads or captures a selfie, and the product reads what actually matters about their coloring — undertone, contrast, depth — and tells them which of the classic seasonal palettes is theirs, then shows the makeup, hair, and clothing colors that will flatter them. Free, they get a real, useful teaser and a shareable card; the full personal report is one-time paid, around $9–15. The audience arrives anxious — 'do I look washed out? why does this color look wrong on me?' — and ready to buy, because they were about to buy makeup anyway. Answer the anxiety fast and honestly."

## Hard lines

- The photo never leaves the device: analysis is fully client-side; the claim must be true at the network layer, not promised in copy.
- The season classifier must be deterministic — same photo, same verdict — and proven by tests.
- Cosmetic guidance only: no medical or diagnosis claims anywhere.
