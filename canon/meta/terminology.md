# Terminology

*The Guide's glossary, verbatim. Each term once, in plain words, with its Guide anchor.*

Guide anchors (G1…G16) refer to the Guide — book eight of the arc, *The Practice* — which
is external canonical text recorded by this spec in the statutes ([../laws/](../laws/)).

**Acceptance criteria.** The checkable statements in a spec that say when the work is right (G9.1).

**Agent.** A machine that reads, writes and runs things on a team's behalf. It takes part in every duty and holds none (G13.1).

**Axiom.** docodego's founding claim, in three lines (G1).

**Cold start.** A test of the record: can a newcomer, or a fresh agent, carry on from the record alone? (G8)

**Declared Omission Rule.** A spec section may be left out only by saying so (G9.1).

**Drift.** Work moving away from its stated intent (G11.3, G14.2).

**Duty.** One of four questions every unit must answer: Author, Architect, Gatekeeper, Governor (G13.1).

**Epic, story, unit.** A problem worth solving; one decision inside it; the smallest piece that can be built, proved and signed (G7.1).

**First-pass acceptance.** The share of units accepted the first time they are shown; one of two live signals (G14.2).

**Gate.** The structural check, then the Intent Clarity Score, that a spec passes before build (G9.2).

**Graduation.** The moment a sketch becomes a unit: real users, real data, a second person, or a signer (G4.2).

**Grilling.** Ten to fifteen hard questions on a spec, from someone other than its author, each answered (G10).

**Halt.** Any signer's power to stop the work, tested before it is relied on (G14.1).

**Intent Clarity Score.** A score out of 100 across completeness, testability, unambiguity and threat coverage (G9.2).

**IRAF.** Intent, Reasoning, Action, Feedback: the steps each unit moves through (G14.2).

**Mode.** hands-on, guided, review or auto: who does the work and who signs (G14.1).

**Movement.** One of Document, Compose, Demonstrate, Govern (G5).

**Patch Spiral.** Fixing output again and again without fixing the intent (G11.3).

**Proof.** Evidence that ran, witnessed, bound to the exact work, checkable by a stranger (G12.1).

**Radar.** The team's list of technologies in four rings: bleeding-edge, stable, conservative, hold (G11.1).

**Receipt.** A unit's last act: proofs, spec version, what it taught, a named signature (G12.2).

**Set down.** An idea put aside on purpose, filed where the next discover will find it (G4).

**Sketch.** Light, one-shot work for exploring, free until it graduates (G4).

**Spec.** Intent compiled into a form a person and an agent can both act on and check (G9.1).

**Statute.** One of sixteen published laws, one per stream (G15).

**Stream.** One of sixteen kinds of work, four per movement (G6).

**Trail.** The append-only record kept beside the work (G8).

## Vocabulary note

The books say **the maker**; the studio's working practice says **founder**. Both mean the
same role here: the person whose answers are recorded verbatim and whose decisions on name,
mission, money and taste are theirs alone (EL-19, EL-31). This spec uses neither word for
the role in its laws — the System names duties, not persons.
