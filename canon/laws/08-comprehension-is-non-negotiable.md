# Statute 8 — Comprehension Is Non-Negotiable

*Movement: Compose · Stream: integrate*

## The law

Published: "If you cannot evaluate what was built, you cannot be accountable for it."

Complete as published.

## Violation

"Approving AI output without reading and understanding it."

## Limits

None in the principle. "Evaluate" does not demand reading every line, so it survives auto
mode, where evaluation runs through evidence and traces. The published detail's monthly and
quarterly practice is better as a trigger: when the first-pass rate falls, or a change
cannot be explained, practise by hand.

## Conformance

Checked today: the reviewer and the author differ on merged work; the review names a person;
the integrate rule holds — nothing is merged that its reviewer cannot explain. Direction:
the tool prompts an explain-back line on the receipt.
