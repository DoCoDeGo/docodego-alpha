# Statute 16 — Alignment Before Demonstration

*Movement: Govern · Stream: measure*

## The law

Published: "No demonstration without governance validation at its required maturity level."

Completion †: "No demonstration without governance validation at its required maturity
level, set by its mode. A sketch shown to learn is not a demonstration."

## Violation

*Merging a demonstration that skipped an alignment check "just this once".*

## Limits

"Maturity level" names stages that modes have replaced as the working control, and
"demonstration" was ambiguous. Showing a sketch to learn needs no governance check; showing
a unit for acceptance does.

## Conformance

Checked today: a demonstration carries proofs whose paths exist (G4); required checks follow
the recorded mode (EL-27). Direction: the tool refuses a demonstration whose mode-required
checks are not recorded.
