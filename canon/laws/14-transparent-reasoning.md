# Statute 14 — Transparent Reasoning

*Movement: Govern · Stream: retrospect*

## The law

Published: "Every agent produces auditable reasoning traces. No black boxes."

Completion † (one word replaced, one clause added): "Every agent produces auditable **action**
traces. No black boxes: audit what it did, not the story it tells."

## Violation

As published: "Accepting agent output without checking the reasoning trace." With the
completion: "Accepting agent output without checking the **action** trace."

## Limits

A model's written reasoning is often not an accurate account of what drove its answer, so
"no black boxes" cannot be honoured for a model's insides. It can be honoured for its
actions. The statute keeps its name: the reasoning that must be transparent is the record's.

## Conformance

Checked today: every tracker claim carries a path to its evidence; every act records whether
a person or an agent did it (EL-25). Direction: the tool links agent actions to their traces
by default.
