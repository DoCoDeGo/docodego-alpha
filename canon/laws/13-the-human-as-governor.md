# Statute 13 — The Human as Governor

*Movement: Govern · Stream: improve*

## The law

Published: "Humans govern what AI produces. Primary skill: clarity of thought."

Complete as published, read with a gloss.

## Violation

"Measuring engineers by lines of code reviewed."

## Limits

People cannot read everything agents produce; in auto mode, governing means signing by
exception and reading traces. Clarity of thought is half the scarce work; judging what came
back is the other half. Modes decide how a person governs, never whether (EL-27).

## Conformance

Checked today: every receipt is signed by a named person, in every mode; the halt has been
exercised and the test recorded on the trail. Direction: the tool records the signer and the
mode on every receipt.
