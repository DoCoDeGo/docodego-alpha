# docodego-finding — the Finding (of the thought)

*Arc phase 1. Mode: pictures and questions — never verdicts, never scores. Integrity question: "Can you stand behind who you are becoming, before there is a thing?"*

The Finding is unfocused looking. Its deliverable is not an idea — it is the **capacity to recognise one**, and a provenance trail for thoughts that arrive. In an age when machines generate unlimited plausible intents, the scarce thing is a thought whose origin you know: it came from a life, not from a feed. This skill defends that.

## When someone is here

Lost, restless, between things, "everything's been done," "I want to do something that matters but don't know what" — or the opposite: a thought keeps returning and wants writing down before it evaporates. Both are Finding. Being lost is a valid location, not a failure state.

## The six modes (postures, not steps)

| Mode | The practice |
|---|---|
| Cultivating | tend the conditions — inputs, rest, breadth — rather than forcing output |
| Living | the life itself is the instrument; problems worth solving are *felt* before they are seen |
| Noticing | catch irritations, workarounds, repeated frictions — yours and others' |
| Apprenticing | go where the problems are; do the work that exposes you to them |
| Listening | what draws you back, unbidden? interest is data |
| Recognising | the moment a noticing and a pull coincide — write it down |

Facilitation: ask, don't assess. Good questions: "What have you noticed three times this month?" · "What do you keep reading about when nobody's making you?" · "What problem have you personally felt that made you angry it wasn't solved?" · "Whose work do you envy, and what specifically?"

## The finding record

One file per recognised thought, in this repository's record layer: `.docodego/01-finding/finding.md`. An idea with no repository yet keeps its record wherever the maker keeps pre-repository ideas, until a repository exists to hold it.

```markdown
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
status: noticing | forming | ready-to-walk
---
# <working name>

## The noticing
What was noticed, concretely. Where and when.

## Provenance
Where this thought came from — the lived source (a felt problem, a job, an
obsession), not the trend that surfaced it. The test: do you know where this
thought came from, and is that source your life?

## The pull
What keeps drawing you back. Interest stated honestly, not justified.

## The becoming
Who does pursuing this begin to make you? Can you stand behind that person?
```

## Rules

- **No evaluation here.** Market size, feasibility, competition — all of it belongs to the Grounding. A finding record judged at birth is a finding record never written.
- **The human answers the integrity question.** Ask it, record the answer verbatim, move on.
- Many records stay `noticing` forever. That is the phase working, not failing.

## Transition

A record moves to `ready-to-walk` when it can state a goal ("what would success be, and at what size?") and a concrete problem-holder. Then → `docodego-grounding`. Never promote a thought straight to a repository — a thought that skips the walk starts its thread broken.
