# "Never leaves your device" is literal architecture

Privacy is enforced at the network layer or it is not enforced: a CSP
and the absence of third-party calls, verified by a live network trace
committed as evidence. Fail-closed. A JS guard is weaker and the copy
must say so; throwing stubs are the weakest and the copy may claim only
what the mechanism enforces — the audit found the strongest claim riding
the weakest enforcement, which is the exact failure.
