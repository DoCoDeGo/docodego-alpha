# Statute 7 — Architecture Before Generation

*Movement: Compose · Stream: build*

## The law

Published: "The human defines system structure before agents begin. AI implements within
the architecture, not instead of it."

Complete as published.

## Violation

"Letting agents begin without a declared system architecture."

## Limits

Agents routinely draft structures, and sketching structure is good practice. "Before agents
begin" applies to build, not to the sketch. Agents may propose structure in a sketch; a
person declares it before build. A small fix inherits its structure.

## Conformance

Checked today: a build unit points at a declared structure; technology choices are checked
against the radar's four rings (a hold choice questions the work; a bleeding-edge choice
moves the unit to a stricter mode). Direction: the tool records who declared the structure
and when.
