# Engine law 19 — Answers recorded verbatim

## The law

When a decision belongs to someone else, ask it with a recommendation: options briefly,
which you recommend and why, then ask. Record the answer exactly as given, without tidying.
"Recording the answer word for word makes them durable: in a year, nobody can argue about
what was said."

## Why

A tidied answer is an interpretation. The record's value is that it holds what the decider
actually said — including the hesitations that turn out to be the decision.

## Violation

A decision entry that paraphrases, corrects or completes the decider's words.

## Conformance

Checked today: human review at every fold. Direction: amendment records quote the sign-off
verbatim beside the hash of the signed text.
