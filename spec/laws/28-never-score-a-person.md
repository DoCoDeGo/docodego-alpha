# Engine law 28 — Never score a person

## The law

The measures are for learning about the work. They never rank, score or compare people or
teams. There are no leaderboards. Telemetry watches the work — never a person: not
keystrokes, not hours, not how long anyone took. Accountability means a named person answers
for the work they signed; surveillance means watching people. The System asks for the first
and forbids the second.

## Why

The moment first-pass acceptance is reported by name, people avoid hard work that might be
sent back, and the signal rises while the work gets worse. The rule keeps the signals true.

## Violation

A dashboard broken down by person; a signal used to compare teams; a receipt quoted as a
performance review.

## Conformance

Checked today: the tool carries no per-person metrics by construction (EL-26). Direction:
when someone asks for names, the written decline exists — offer the signal by part of the
work, with the actions taken when it crossed its line, and record the exchange on the trail.
