# Engine law 31 — Owner-only decisions go to the owner

*Pillar: govern — some questions only the owner of the work answers.*

## The law

Some decisions belong to the owner of the work and to nobody else: what the product is for,
what it is called, what it costs, what it must never become. Research and agents can inform
these. They cannot settle them. When you notice one, do not guess: write the question, with
what you have learned and a recommendation, and ask. "It is slower by a day and faster by a
year, because nobody builds for months on a guess."

## Why

An agent that guesses a naming, pricing or mission question has not saved time; it has
manufactured a future the owner never chose. The studio's law — founder-integrity questions
go to the founder — is this law at the studio's scale.

## Violation

A tool or agent settling what the product costs; a research file standing in for an answer
nobody gave.

## Conformance

Checked today: human review; the question and its recorded answer follow EL-19 (verbatim).
Direction: the record conventions mark owner-only questions as a distinct record type so
they cannot be auto-closed.
