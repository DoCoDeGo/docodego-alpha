# Engine law 30 — The Anti-Complexity Principle

*Pillar: govern — the test every addition to the System must pass.*

## The law

No addition may increase net ceremony unless the increase is proportional to demonstrated
risk from what machines do unchecked. Governance scales with autonomy, not with
organisational size. It is the test every step must pass, not permission to skip one.

The practice should also teach itself out of a job: as habits form, the tool and the
checklist should matter less.

## Why

Ceremony is how governance dies — not by being rejected but by being tolerated. Every check,
record or signal that cannot name the risk it answers is a tax on the units that were doing
fine.

## Violation

A new required record that answers no demonstrated risk; a rule with no test behind it
promoted from a single sighting.

## Conformance

Checked today: human review at every addition — "an action with no owner, or a rule whose
test has never been seen failing, is not yet an improvement." Direction: canon coverage
surfaces gates that enforce no law (orphans) and laws with no gate, so ceremony has nowhere
to hide.
