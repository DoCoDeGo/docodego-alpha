> # Engine law 33 — Statuses move through gates
>
> *Pillar: record — the rails the loop runs on are record, not convention.*
>
> ## The law
>
> A delivery unit's status is written by the gate that earned it, and by
> nothing else. The chain is draft → grilled → scored → delivered, and
> each step's command — grill, score, deliver — sets the status when its
> own gate passes and records the evidence beside it (the grill round,
> the score breakdown, the delivered receipt). Retirement and withdrawal
> are commands too: retirement moves the unit to its tomb and stamps it
> (`retired_on`, `was`) — it does not mint a new status value. A status
> that moved backward or forward without its gate's record on file is
> not a status: it is a rewritten record (EL-17), refused.
>
> ## Why
>
> Statuses are the loop's rails — every rollup, gate, and audit reads
> them as facts. A status that a hand can set is a fact a hand can
> forge: draft work presented as delivered skips every check that
> delivers guard. The rails hold because the writer is the checker.
>
> ## Violation
>
> Frontmatter edited from draft to delivered by hand; a delivered unit
> with no grill round or score on file; a unit un-delivered by editing
> its frontmatter instead of recording why.
>
> ## Conformance
>
> Checked today: the CLI commands are the only writers (grill, score,
> deliver, retire each act through their gates, evidence recorded
> beside); `check` re-reads statuses against the gate records — a
> delivered unit without its delivered.md is a finding; the audit's
> GOV-003 cross-checks delivered units against their grill verdicts.
> Direction: a status transition table enforced by check — every gate-
> less move refused by the machine, not just detectable — specified as
> a delivery unit in the record's debt map, landing with its own unit.

## What changed

A new engine law is added at EL-33 (veto, record pillar), carrying the
status-chain principle with its enforceability named: which gate sets
which status (grill → grilled, score → scored, deliver → delivered,
retire → the tomb, stamped not re-statussed), what refuses a gate-less
move today (nothing mechanized — detection only, named honestly), and
what will refuse it (the transition table specified in the record's
debt map, landing with its own unit). This draft is independent of the
EL-18 amendment in the same batch: separate files, separate sign-offs;
whichever is accepted flips with the version bump, the other stays
draft.

## Why

The session's statuses did the work of law without being law: every
gate that matters (grill verdicts, the score floor, the deliver gate,
retirement) writes status as a side effect of checking, and the record's
audit machinery already treats status-without-evidence as a finding.
Promoting the practice pins it before a future tool or a hurried hand
edits frontmatter around the gates. The EL-30 risk answered: a record
whose statuses can be forged is a record whose rollups, audits, and
launch gates all read fiction.

## What risk it answers

Status forgery — the cheapest attack on the record (one frontmatter
line turns draft into delivered) — gets a named law with its enforcement
surface stated exactly: what checks today, what refuses tomorrow, and
which unit carries the refusal. Honest about the gap: the law ships as
detection (check + GOV-003) with refusal specified and scheduled, not
pretended.

## Grilling (non-author, 2026-11-08)

Ten questions from a reader who did not write the draft; answers on the
record, revisions listed below.

1. Backward moves are "refused" but nothing refuses them today — why
   ship the law now rather than with the transition table? — The duty
   has real enforcement today: single-writer (the CLI is the only
   status writer, true now), detection (check cross-reads statuses
   against markers; GOV-003 cross-checks delivered against grill).
   Refusal-at-move is named as the landing commitment with its unit.
   Pretended enforcement is the only dishonest option; this draft
   avoids it.
2. One chain for units, epics, and documents alike? — Revision
   applied: the law now scopes itself to "a delivery unit's status";
   documents walk the same commands. Sketches are a discovery inbox,
   not statuses.
3. "The CLI is the only writer" — does that forbid the founder
   editing their own record? EL-31 (owner-only decisions) cuts the
   other way. — No: EL-31 governs decisions; the owner decides whether
   to deliver, the CLI writes the delivery. Hand-editing bypasses the
   gate evidence and is forgery regardless of whose hand it is — the
   honesty law binds the owner first.
4. Is "retired" a status? The tomb's frontmatter keeps status
   delivered. — Revision applied — the draft as written implied a new
   status value; corrected: retirement moves the unit and stamps the
   tomb (`retired_on`, `was`), it does not mint a status.
5. What refuses a gate-less forward move (draft → delivered)? —
   Deliver's own G3 (a passing grill verdict on file) refuses it today;
   the law states the invariant the gates collectively implement, so a
   refactor cannot quietly drop one.
6. The Direction line cites work that doesn't exist — an EL-18
   violation inside a law draft? — Revision applied: the line names the
   debt-map specification, not a unit number; the commitment is
   recorded where its spec will land (the loop: spec + grill before
   code).
7. Does "statuses" cover ring values, codeState, and the other status-
   like fields in the reference layer? — No: those are reference-layer
   fields with their own writers (init, upgrade). Revision applied: the
   law scopes to the delivery unit's status.
8. severity: veto with only partial detection — precedent? — EL-18
   itself: veto with a lint direction only. The checked-today /
   direction split is the established pattern; detection is real
   enforcement, honestly labelled.
9. Honest rework sometimes needs a backward move (delivered → grilled
   again after a setback) — does "never backward" block that? — Rework
   runs a NEW round recorded on top (append law): the gates re-set the
   status forward by their own authority. What is refused is a move
   without its gate. Revision applied: the law says "backward or
   forward without its gate's record", not a bare "never backward".
10. Two amendments, one version — what if the founder accepts one and
    declines the other? — They are independent: separate files,
    separate sign-offs; the version bump carries whichever land
    together. Revision applied: the independence is now stated in
    What changed of both files.
